# Demo content and provenance

The images were generated with OpenAI image-generation tools and selected, reviewed and curated by Tong Yaohui. They are not photographs of actual locations, film stills or editable 3D models. Names such as “Film still realism” and “Nature documentary realism” describe visual directions, not the source of the images.

The original PNG bytes and embedded C2PA provenance data are retained. The provenance.json file records IDs, SHA256 hashes, dimensions, source evidence and review results. Embedded provenance markers were read, but the certificate signature chain was not independently verified; markers alone are not proof of ownership. No global reverse-image search or exhaustive comparison against third-party works was performed.
